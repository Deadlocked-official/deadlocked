Click targets as fast as you can. Press Esc to stop early.

Flick

One target at a time in a random spot. Trains snapping onto heroes that jump into view. Score counts hits; reaction is the average time from spawn to hit.

Multi

Three targets stay up; each hit spawns a new one. Trains moving between targets in a team fight.

Tracking

Hold the mouse button while the cursor is on a target that strafes and dashes. Trains staying on a moving hero with automatic weapons.

Headshots

Hero-shaped targets that sidestep. Head hits score 3, body hits 1. Deadlock rewards headshots with bonus damage on most guns.
